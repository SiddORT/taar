---
name: Client account order visibility
description: Automatic non-draft order visibility is separate from publishing client-facing content and bearer links.
---

Clients should automatically see their own non-deleted, non-draft swatch and style orders, including completed and cancelled orders with their actual statuses.

**Why:** The user approved separating account visibility from manual publication after both New Balance and Zara appeared empty despite having ERP orders.

**How to apply:** Do not restore published-only filtering for account lists, dashboard totals, or basic order details. Never broaden access across client ownership boundaries. Publishing remains necessary for artwork, images, conversations, and bearer-link access; unpublishing hides that content, not the basic account order.

Keep existing link-based identifiers and conversation histories compatible. Any internal link records needed to represent account orders must stay unpublished until staff explicitly publishes them.

**Why:** The existing public links and conversations already use those identifiers. Automatically making orders visible must not inadvertently enable bearer-link access or advance their workflow status.

**How to apply:** Treat automatic account representation as bookkeeping, not publication. Do not email clients, change order statuses, or enable public links merely because an account views its order list.
